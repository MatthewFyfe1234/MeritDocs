# DSLIB IT: limitations

1. **No worker identity across projects.** Each activity records which employee did it, but employee letters are per project. Employee A in C2015-15 is not known to be Employee A in C2015-20. Experience cannot accumulate across projects, so eligibility cannot be computed.

2. **No scaling descriptors on spec nodes.** Nothing records the size or scope of what was delivered, so effort and duration cannot be scaled from one project to another. This rules out forecasting.

3. **No record of choice.** The data records who did a step, not who else could have done it or why they took it. Self-selection can only be inferred from the outcome.
