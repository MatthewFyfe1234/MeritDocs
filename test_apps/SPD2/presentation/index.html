    What to do

    Choose a dataset from the tabs. Hover over a node of the master specification to see the steps it necessitates, or over a step to see the nodes that necessitate it. Tick the box beside a node to add it to a new project: its steps light up in the master process as you go. Click a node to see its descriptors below the tree, and enter their values there. Once the project has its forecast, press Play, at the left of the master process, to run it, or Autoplay to have projects drawn at random and run in a loop: its agents take their contracts and its steps execute, to delivery. Click Play again while it plays to play it faster. Or press Step to play the next step at a time, ticking more nodes between steps to grow the project; a node whose steps would come before ones already played is refused, those steps crossed out. Step never closes the project: once its steps so far are played, it waits to grow, and Play delivers it. Reset goes back to before the project was first played. Open Projects to see past projects, or to delete one.
